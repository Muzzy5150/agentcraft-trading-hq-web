# Minecraft policy review — 2026-10-22

Reviewed primary sources:

- https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula
- https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines

The EULA restricts distribution/commercial use of Mojang-created material except as permitted. The Usage Guidelines distinguish distributing an original mod from distributing a modded game, and prohibit mods that create play-to-earn functionality involving real-world or cash-out currency. Branding must avoid implying official affiliation. These requirements inform the no-game-binaries packaging boundary, prominent independent-project disclaimer and paper-only operation here.

A future real-money service must be evaluated independently against the then-current EULA, Usage Guidelines, platform rules and applicable law. Separating signing from Minecraft does not itself establish compliance. No claim of Mojang approval or legal clearance is made. This is an implementation review, not a legal opinion.
